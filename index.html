Structural & Material Engineering

01

Titanium Reinforcement

High-strength titanium alloys integrated into load-bearing frames extend airframe operational lifespan to 4,000 flight hours (approx. 30 years service life).

02

RCS Signature Reduction

Frontal Radar Cross Section (RCS) reduced to 1–3 m² using Radar-Absorbing Materials (RAM) coatings and optimized engine inlet duct geometries.

03

Aerodynamic Refinement

Eliminated canards from earlier Su-35 prototypes; modern digital Fly-By-Wire (FBW) controls all aerodynamic surfaces for high-angle-of-attack stability.

N035 Passive Electronically Scanned Array radar capable of operating across multiple frequencies.

  • 400 km Detection Range against 3m² aerial targets
  • 90-100 km Detection for low-RCS / stealth targets

Nose-mounted Infrared Search & Track (IRST) sensor for passive, stealthy target acquisition.

  • 50-90 km Passive Range via thermal signatures
  • Integrated Laser Rangefinder & Target Designator
  • Zero RF emission detection (Silent Tracking)
